# British Columbia Regulatory & Compliance Guide

This document outlines the required steps for Road Ready's formal business establishment and ongoing compliance in British Columbia.

## 1. Incorporation Checklist (B.C. Limited Company)

- [ ] **Name Request:** Submit a Name Request through BC Registries. "Road Ready" or a variation like "Road Ready Marketplace Ltd."
- [ ] **Establish Articles of Incorporation:** Use Table 1 articles or draft custom ones.
- [ ] **Incorporation Agreement:** Signed by all initial directors/shareholders.
- [ ] **File Incorporation Application:** Via BC Registry Services (approx. $350 fee).
- [ ] **Register with CRA:** Obtain a 9-digit Business Number (BN) (automatically triggered by BC incorporation in most cases).
- [ ] **Corporate Records Office:** Establish a physical or digital minute book.

## 2. Tax Registrations

### GST (Federal)
- **Mandatory:** If gross revenue exceeds $30,000 in a 12-month period.
- **Voluntary:** Recommended early to claim Input Tax Credits (ITCs) on startup expenses.
- **Action:** Register via CRA My Business Account.

### PST (Provincial)
- **Applicability:** Driving lessons are generally PST-exempt in BC, but the *marketplace platform fee* may be subject to PST if classified as a software service.
- **Action:** Consult with a BC tax professional to determine if "Marketplace Software" fees require PST collection.

## 3. WorkSafeBC (Occupational Health & Safety)

### Mandatory Registration
- **Threshold:** Any business with one or more employees (including active shareholders) must register.
- **Independent Contractors:** While instructors are independent contractors, WorkSafeBC may deem them "workers" if they work exclusively for the platform.
- **Strategy:** Road Ready should register and potentially pay premiums for its core team, and require instructors to maintain their own WorkSafeBC Personal Optional Protection (POP) or confirm their independent status.
- **Action:** Register online at [worksafebc.com](https://www.worksafebc.com).

## 4. PIPA Compliance (Privacy)

- **Privacy Officer:** Designate a Privacy Officer (standard startup practice).
- **Consent:** Ensure the app explicitly captures consent for collecting Driver's License images and Telematics data.
- **Breach Protocol:** Document a simple process for notifying users and the BC OIPC in case of a data breach.

## 5. Instructor Compliance Requirements

Instructors must provide:
1. **ICBC Instructor Certificate:** Proof of valid certification.
2. **Commercial Insurance:** Proof of "Driver Training" endorsement on their vehicle insurance.
3. **Criminal Record Check:** Periodic checks (ICBC requirement).
