# Initial Concept
A platform connecting driving instructors with students, featuring a web-based management system and a mobile application for on-the-go access to progress tracking and educational resources.

# Product Definition: Road Ready

## Vision
Road Ready aims to modernize the driving education experience by providing a seamless, digital connection between students and professional instructors. It replaces traditional paper logs and fragmented communication with a centralized hub for scheduling, progress tracking, and educational materials.

## Target Users
*   **Student Drivers:** Individuals seeking to obtain their driver's license who need to find, book, and track their lessons and progress.
*   **Professional Driving Instructors:** Certified instructors looking to manage their business, track student performance, and automate administrative tasks.

## Core Goals
*   **For Students:** Simplify the process of finding qualified instructors, booking lessons within budget, and preparing for the road test through interactive tools like quizzes and digital handbooks.
*   **For Instructors:** Streamline lesson management, provide data-driven feedback through diagnostic evaluations, and ensure timely payouts.

## Key Features
*   **Student & Instructor Portals:** Dedicated web and mobile interfaces tailored to each user type's needs.
*   **Automated Scheduling & Booking:** A real-time system for managing availability and lesson reservations.
*   **Educational Ecosystem:** Interactive driving handbooks, practice quizzes, and progress logs to ensure students are "Road Ready."
*   **Diagnostic Evaluations:** Specialized tools for instructors to perform and log diagnostic rides, providing students with clear areas for improvement. Now enhanced with HMM-based map matching for precise route alignment.
*   **Live Ride Monitoring:** Real-time visualization of diagnostic rides, allowing instructors to see telemetry, location, and driving events as they happen. Includes real-time safety zone alerts (School & Playground) based on OSM metadata.
*   **Smart Navigation (Planned):** Intelligent routing for lessons using OSM data to optimize for safety and efficiency.
*   **Real-time Communication:** Built-in messaging to facilitate coordination between students and instructors.
*   **Financial Management:** Automated payout tracking and financial reporting for instructors.
