# Product Guidelines: Road Ready

## Tone and Voice
*   **Professional and Trustworthy:** As a platform dealing with road safety and professional certification, all communications must be clear, accurate, and instill confidence in both students and instructors.
*   **Encouraging and Supportive:** Learning to drive can be stressful. The UI and messaging should be helpful, guiding users through complex tasks with empathy.
*   **Direct and Transparent:** Avoid jargon. Be clear about pricing, schedules, and progress status.

## Visual Identity
*   **Clean and Accessible:** Prioritize high contrast, legible typography (e.g., Sans-serif fonts), and a layout that works well on both mobile and desktop.
*   **Safety-Focused Palette:** Use a professional color scheme (e.g., Deep Blues and Grays) with clear accent colors for actions (e.g., Road-sign Green or Yellow) and warnings.
*   **Intuitive Navigation:** Users should be able to access their dashboard, schedule, or handbook with minimal clicks.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
*   **Mobile-First for Students:** Students will primarily use the app for quick checks and logging. Ensure the mobile experience is fast and responsive.
*   **Efficiency for Instructors:** Instructors are often on the move. Minimize data entry for logging sessions and diagnostic rides.
*   **Clarity of Progress:** Progress should always be visible (e.g., progress bars, checklist completions) to motivate students and provide instructors with immediate context.
*   **Reliability:** Offline support or graceful degradation for the mobile app is critical, as instructors may be in areas with poor connectivity during rides.
