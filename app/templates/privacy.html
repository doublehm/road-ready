Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Road Ready ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal information in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of British Columbia and other applicable Canadian privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide our services and ensure safety on our platform:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password (stored using secure hashing).
  • Identity Verification: Driver's license numbers, expiration dates, and images of the physical license. This is strictly for verifying that Instructors and Students are legally permitted to drive/teach.
  • Transaction Data: Payment history and payout details (processed securely via Stripe; we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers).
  • Location Data: Pickup and drop-off locations for lessons.
  • Telematics Data: For the "Diagnostic Ride" feature, we collect accelerometer, gyroscope, and GPS data from your mobile device to provide objective driving scores.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Connect Students with appropriate Instructors.
  • Verify identity and professional credentials to ensure safety.
  • Process payments and facilitate payouts.
  • Improve our driving evaluation algorithms.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared in the following circumstances:

  • Between Users: Limited information (name, pickup location) is shared between the Student and Instructor to facilitate the lesson.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third parties like Stripe for payment processing and cloud providers for secure hosting.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or to report a safety incident to ICBC or law enforcement.

5. Data Security and Retention

We use industry-standard physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your data. This includes SSL encryption for data in transit and bcrypt hashing for passwords.

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the fulfillment of the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

Under PIPA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent for data collection at any time (subject to legal or contractual restrictions).
